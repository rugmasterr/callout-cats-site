The collection

Callout Cats is 1,111 hand-built 32×32 pixel cats. Every cat is one of four breeds, one for each platform where the coin gets called out, and is dressed from a wardrobe of backgrounds, hats, eyewear and outfits, so no two come out the same.

The rules

  • One cat per wallet.
  • Buy at least $10 of the coin before you call out.
  • Your callout must contain only your EVM wallet address. Anything else in the message and it won't be picked up.
  • Callouts only count on gmgn, pump.fun, Axiom or FOMO.
  • 1,111 cats total. When the grid is full, the mint is over.

Callout Cats is a collectible art project. Nothing here is financial advice, and buying the coin comes with the usual risk of losing what you put in. Never share your seed phrase; minting only ever needs your public address.
